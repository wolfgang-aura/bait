BAIT checks the trader before your AI agent sends money.

Your agent proposes an amount. BAIT sends all of it or none of it. It reads the trader's 7-day and 30-day record from Nansen and holds the money on a losing month, a week that contradicts the month, a thin sample or a low win rate.
