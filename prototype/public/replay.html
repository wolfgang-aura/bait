    3Guard

    One Nansen call · no model tools · fail closed

    • Checks wallet, 30-day window, source, freshness and realised PnL.
    • Negative, missing, stale or mismatched evidence forces $0.
    • The model proposes. BAIT decides what execution receives.
    Built forAI agents, copy platforms and managed vaults
    ProtectsCapital allocations to perpetual-trading wallets
    ReturnsAllow or block, enforced amount, reason and evidence
    Does not doPick wallets, predict returns or execute trades
