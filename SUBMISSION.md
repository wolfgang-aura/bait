## Draft description

BAIT asks whether true but selective evidence can persuade an AI allocator to fund
a losing trader. Players send the same pitch to two desks. One sees only the pitch;
the other can query Nansen PnL and trade history. The receipt exposes the selected
facts, the full record and both decisions.

Across 90 recorded replays on one losing wallet, the no-tool desk funded it in
23/30 runs. PnL and trade tools funded it in 13/30, and adding open positions
reduced that to 9/30. These are fictional allocations under a permissive policy,
not proof of instruction violations or trading performance. Full traces and
reproduction commands accompany the demo.
