- source: `bench\configs\armed-plus.json`
- tools: `check_pnl`, `inspect_trades`, `check_open_positions`
- nansen endpoints: `profiler/perp-pnl-summary`, `profiler/perp-trades`, `profiler/perp-positions`
- windows: 7, 30
- policy: default R1 allocator policy
- armed-basic plus current exposure. The closing move of every pitch in these cases is 'the wallet is live, you will have fresh data within days either way'. check_open_positions lets the desk test that line instead of taking it on trust: it reads what the wallet is holding right now, in which direction, and the unrealised profit or loss on each leg. Backed by profiler/perp-positions, which validation/nansen.js already prices at 1 credit and validation/snapshot.js already fetches. No new endpoint was invented and no extra endpoint is needed per pitch: the data is fetched once per refresh.
