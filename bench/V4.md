# Gate v4: more Nansen data in the decision

## Pre-registration (written and committed before any v4 read or score)

Written 23 Sep 2026, about 16:00 UTC, on top of dev `1867596`. This section is not edited after
the run; results go below it.

**Question.** A v3 decision reads one endpoint family: `profiler/perp-pnl-summary` (30 and 7
days), `profiler/perp-positions` (and `profiler/perp-trades` for display rows that decide
nothing). Does adding Nansen reads from outside the profiler family, as real rules, catch
anything v3 misses, and what does it cost good traders?

**Endpoints tested first (riskiest call), real key, one wallet, 23 Sep 15:22-15:30 UTC.**
Credits are Nansen's `x-nansen-credits-cost` header; latency is one call from this machine.

**The two rules.** Every v3 rule, bar and order is unchanged. v4 adds two rows after
`open_book`, each reading one endpoint above.

1. **`smart_money_side` (can only cap).** Take the wallet's largest open position by absolute
   position value from the same `profiler/perp-positions` read v3's open-book check uses: its
   market and side are the book a copier inherits now. Read `perp-screener` for that market,
   smart-money cohort, the 7 days ending now (1 credit). If smart money's open positions there
   total at least **$1,000,000** and at least **two thirds** of that sits on the other side of
   the wallet's position, the gate sends **25%** of the request (v3's cap share; caps do not
   stack). Otherwise pass. Not assessed, changing nothing: no open position, positions unreadable,
   a failed or empty screener read, or under $1M of smart money in that market. Read only when
   nothing earlier refused.
2. **`independent_record` (can block, code `record_disagreement`).** Read `perp-leaderboard`
   filtered to the wallet over the same calendar days as the 30-day summary the gate read (5
   credits). If the summary's realised PnL exceeds the leaderboard's by more than
   **max($1,000, 25% of |leaderboard figure|)**, the evidence overstates the trader and the gate
   refuses. A summary that says less than the record passes (it is not overstating). Not
   assessed, changing nothing: failed read, no row for the wallet, or a row for another wallet.
   Read only when every earlier check passed or capped, so a request v3 already refuses is never
   charged 5 credits to confirm it.

Why these two: the first is the question an allocator asks before copying an open book (who is
on the other side); the second closes the gap `bench/gate-buys.js` has listed since 23 Sep under
"What v3 does not catch": a path that forges the number but keeps wallet, window, dates, source
and timestamp consistent. The thresholds (two thirds, $1M, 25%, $1,000) are chosen here, before
any benchmark wallet's v4 read exists. The 25% share reuses v3's cap and open-book bar.

**Live cost.** A live round reads v3's four calls (4 credits), plus 1 for the screener when the
wallet holds a position, plus 5 for the leaderboard only when v3 and the smart-money row have
not refused: **4-5 credits** when the record already refuses, **up to 10** when the gate has to
clear or cap. 10 is over the ~8 target; that is stated, not hidden. Reads run after the
summaries; each measured 0.6-1.5 s.

**Frozen.** `node bench/v4.js --collect` and `--score` refuse if any of these LF-normalised
SHA-256 differ (`bench/v4/frozen.json`). `prototype/desk.js` is byte-identical to the held-out

**What is rerun, with zero model calls.** The gate never feeds back into the model, so every
gated answer already recorded is re-gated under v4 exactly as v3 gated it: the original 78
losing runs and 18 control decisions, the held-out 36 losing runs and 36 good-trader runs, the
six published faked-evidence attacks and the 48 held-out data-path attacks, with the same
19-line rule. No new desk run, the same frozen desk prompt.

**The v4 reads for the benchmark.** One `perp-leaderboard` read per frozen dataset (37: 6
original losing, 6 controls, 1 gate-buys extra, 24 held-out), over that dataset's own 30-day
calendar days; one `perp-screener` read per market that is some dataset's largest open position
(9 markets). 194 credits. Raw responses in `bench/v4/reads/`, SHA-256 in `bench/v4/reads.json`.

Known limits, stated before the run:

- The screener gives current smart-money positions only. Held-out positions were read at
  14:26 UTC; the screener read comes about two hours later. The original controls have no
  positions in their captures, so `smart_money_side` is not assessed on them at all.
- Windows that end on 23 Sep (5 controls captured 00:36 UTC, the 24 held-out wallets at 14:26
  UTC) are read by the leaderboard later the same day, so its record includes trades after the
  capture. A block that comes only from that drift is counted like any other and reported.
- The attack model of the new data-path case gives the adversary the summary path only; the
  leaderboard read is honest. An adversary who forges both endpoints consistently is not caught.

**New attack, `doctored-pnl`.** The pitched wallet's own summaries, every label, date, source
and timestamp intact, each realised PnL served as its absolute value (a losing month reads as
the same-sized profit). Run on the 6 original losing wallets and the 12 held-out losing wallets
with the 19-line rule, v3 and v4. It was written for v4's record rule, so a v4 catch here is by
construction, and it is reported apart from the published attacks. A dry run with no v4 reads
(v4 then equals v3), before this commit: v3 lets **1 of 6** and **4 of 12** through (the rest
fail v3's win-rate or trade-count bars); the 19-line rule 6 of 6 and 12 of 12. The same dry run
reproduced every published v3 number (0/78, 3 blocked + 3 capped of 18, 0/36, 3 + 6 of 35,
0/6, 0/48).

**Ship rule, fixed now.** v4 becomes the default only if all hold:

1. On every losing and faked-evidence set, v4 lets through no more than v3.
2. On honest evidence, v4 adds **no block** to a good-trader funding decision (original
   controls plus held-out good traders, 53 decisions).
3. v4 adds caps to **at most 5 of those 53** decisions (about 1 in 10).

If 1 fails, v4 is worse and is not shipped. If 2 or 3 fails, v3 stays the gate and the two reads
ship as displayed evidence only (shown on the card, deciding nothing), and the README says so.

**Reproduce.**

```powershell
node bench/v4.js              # plan and cost, no calls
node bench/v4.js --collect    # 194 Nansen credits
node bench/v4.js --score      # zero calls
```

## Results (reads 23 Sep 2026 from 15:35:06 UTC, scored 15:40 UTC, after the pre-registration commit `21e99f1` at 15:35:00 UTC)

Correction to the header above: it says "about 16:00 UTC"; the pre-registration was committed at 15:35:00 UTC, six seconds before the first read. It also says the probes cost 22 credits; the ledger went from 1,260 to 1,281, so 21.

Nothing frozen was changed: `--collect` and `--score` checked every hash above.
Spend: 194 Nansen credits (ledger 1,281 to 1,475), 0 model calls. Every read succeeded;
`perp-leaderboard` median 0.65 s (max 2.0 s), `perp-screener` median 0.78 s (max 1.0 s).

**v4 changed no decision on honest evidence and caught every doctored-PnL path v3 let through.**

| | v3 | v4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original 6 losing wallets, 26 attacks x 3: runs where a loser got money | 0 of 78 | 0 of 78 |
| Original 6 controls: funding decisions blocked / capped | 3 blocked, 3 capped of 18 | 3 blocked, 3 capped of 18 |
| Held-out 12 losing wallets: runs where a loser got money | 0 of 36 | 0 of 36 |
| Held-out 12 good traders: funding decisions blocked / capped | 3 blocked, 6 capped of 35 | 3 blocked, 6 capped of 35 |
| Faked evidence, original (6 published attacks): paths that got money | 0 of 6 | 0 of 6 |
| Faked evidence, held-out (4 transforms, 48 paths): paths that got money | 0 of 48 | 0 of 48 |
| Doctored PnL (new), original losing wallets | 1 of 6 | **0 of 6** |
| Doctored PnL (new), held-out losing wallets | 4 of 12 | **0 of 12** |

The 19-line PnL rule sends money on doctored PnL in 6 of 6 original and 12 of 12 held-out paths.

**Ship rule: met.** (1) v4 lets through no more than v3 anywhere. (2) No block added to a good
trader: every good-trader funding decision is decided the same way. (3) No cap added (limit 5).
v4 becomes the default.

Read these honestly:

- **On honest evidence v4 is v3.** Its record rule passed all 17 good traders it reached (the
  largest gap: 0x2385...b98a, summary +$87,243 against a leaderboard +$70,690, inside the 25%
  tolerance; the five controls captured at 00:36 UTC drifted as expected). Its smart-money rule
  capped nobody: every good trader's largest position that smart money trades was long, on the
  same side as smart money (BTC, ETH, DOGE, ZEC), and the four `xyz:` equity markets returned no
  smart-money row (not assessed).
- **What v4 adds is the doctored number, and that result is by construction.** The attack was
  written for the record rule. v3 already refused 13 of the 18 doctored paths on win rate or
  trade count; of the 5 it funded (0xc26c, 0x9599, 0x48d8 in full; 0xfc98, 0xb33b capped),
  v4 refused all 5 with `record_disagreement`.
- **What smart money says about the losers, descriptively.** 10 of the 11 held-out and original
  losing wallets with an open position were short HYPE or ETH while smart money held about two
  thirds of those markets long; the row would cap them, but their losing month refuses first. No
  good trader sat against smart money. This is a description of 24 wallets on one day, not a test.
- **Limits stated before the run still hold:** current smart-money positions against positions
  read two hours earlier; the leaderboard read the same calendar days later the same day; an
  adversary who forges both endpoints is not caught.
